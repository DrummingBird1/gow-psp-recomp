GoWLauncher.exe: third-party components

The launcher itself is GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE). It contains:

- Qt 6 and PySide6 (Qt for Python), The Qt Company Ltd., used under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3
  (LGPL-3.0). Qt and PySide6 are linked dynamically: the Qt/PySide6 DLLs are separate files that the launcher
  unpacks next to its Python runtime at start, and can be replaced with your own builds of the same version.
  Source code: https://code.qt.io/cgit/qt/ and https://code.qt.io/cgit/pyside/pyside-setup.git/
  (or https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/). The LGPL-3.0 text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt
- Python (PSF License), https://www.python.org/
- Pillow (MIT-CMU License), https://python-pillow.org/
- Cinzel font, Copyright 2020 The Cinzel Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt next to this file).
- PyInstaller bootloader (GPL-2.0 with the bootloader exception).

At build time, CMake downloads further third-party sources (SDL3, libchdr, OpenH264, PPSSPP's at3_standalone,
libkirk and PrxDecrypter); they keep their own licenses and are not part of the launcher.
Portable build tools. On the first Build, after asking, the launcher downloads these from the projects' official
GitHub releases (checked against pinned SHA-256 hashes) into GoW_PSP_data\build\toolchain. They are not part of the
launcher download, and each folder keeps its own license files:
- llvm-mingw 20261006 (https://github.com/mstorsjo/llvm-mingw): LLVM, Clang, lld, libc++, compiler-rt under the
  Apache License 2.0 with LLVM Exceptions; mingw-w64 headers and runtime (https://www.mingw-w64.org/) under the
  Zope Public License 2.1, the MIT license and public-domain terms (see its LICENSE files). The games built with it
  link parts of the mingw-w64 runtime and the LLVM runtime libraries statically; both licenses allow that without
  extra conditions on the result (the LLVM Exceptions cover compiled code).
- CMake 4.4.4 (https://cmake.org/), BSD 3-Clause License.
- Ninja 1.13.2 (https://ninja-build.org/), Apache License 2.0.

Texture upscaler. When the player asks for an AI-upscaled texture pack, after asking, the launcher downloads
Real-ESRGAN ncnn Vulkan 0.2.5.0 (https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN, BSD 3-Clause License; ncnn, BSD
3-Clause) from its official GitHub release, checked against a pinned SHA-256, into GoW_PSP_data\build\tools. It is
not part of the download.
